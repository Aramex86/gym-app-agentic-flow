2 · Cost, to the pennyCap is your choice (env). Unset = honest unlimited. Set = hard stop with the budget sheet — before the most expensive hop, including --fix.ADR-0004 · ST-23
3 · Secrets only via envNever printed, logged or prompted. No key = “missing secret” stop before spawn. Copilot rail: your company login is the auth; no key asked.ADR-0007
